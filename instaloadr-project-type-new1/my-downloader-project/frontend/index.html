How to Download Instagram Photos

Follow these steps to save a public Instagram photo or carousel post at full resolution:

  1. Open the post. Find the photo, or multi-photo carousel, you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share icon. It's the paper-plane icon below the post.
  3. Tap "Copy Link." This copies the post's direct URL to your clipboard.
  4. Paste the link above. Come back to this page and paste it into the box at the top.
  5. Tap Download. InstaLoadr lists every photo or video in the post, frame by frame.
  6. Tap Save on each frame. Every image downloads at its original resolution, no recompression.

Works the same way for single photos and multi-image carousels, on iPhone, Android, and desktop — see the tips below for device-specific steps.

Download Instagram photos on any device

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the post in the Instagram app, tap the ⋯ or Share icon, then Copy Link.
  2. Open Safari (or your usual browser) and go back to this page.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download.
  4. Tap Download on the photo, then use the share sheet to save it to Photos.

On Android

  1. Open the post in the Instagram app and tap Copy Link from the share menu.
  2. Switch to Chrome (or your usual browser) and open this page.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download.
  4. Tap Download on the photo — it saves straight to your Downloads folder or Gallery.
