How to Download Instagram Videos

Follow these steps to save a public Instagram video or IGTV upload in its original quality:

  1. Open the post. Find the video or IGTV upload you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share icon. It's the paper-plane icon below the post.
  3. Tap "Copy Link." This copies the post's direct URL to your clipboard.
  4. Paste the link above. Come back to this page and paste it into the box at the top.
  5. Tap Download. InstaLoadr resolves the original MP4 in a few seconds.
  6. Tap Save. The video downloads to your device at full quality, no re-encoding.

The same steps work on iPhone, Android, and desktop — device-specific tips are below.

  4. Tap Save, then use the share sheet to save the video to Photos.
