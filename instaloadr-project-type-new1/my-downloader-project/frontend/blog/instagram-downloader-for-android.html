Instagram Downloader for Android

You don't need to install anything from the Play Store to save Instagram content on Android — a browser-based downloader does the job in a few taps and avoids the permissions and ads that come with a lot of downloader apps.

Downloading on Android, step by step

  1. Open Instagram and find the post, Reel, or Story you want.
  2. Tap the share icon and choose "Copy Link."
  3. Open InstaLoadr in Chrome (or your default Android browser) and paste the link into the matching tool — Reels, Video, Photo, or Story.
  4. Tap Download. Android routes the file straight into your device's Downloads folder.

Where do downloaded files go?

By default, Android saves browser downloads to the Downloads folder, visible in the Files app or your gallery app for photos and videos. If you can't find a file, check your browser's own download history — usually in the menu under "Downloads."

Why skip the app-store downloader apps?

Many Android "Instagram downloader" apps ask for broad storage or account permissions and bundle intrusive ads. Since InstaLoadr runs as a website, it never asks for Instagram login credentials and doesn't need any special permissions beyond what your browser already has.

Works the same on iPhone

If you're switching between devices, the exact same paste-a-link process works on iPhone too.
