How to Download Instagram Reels

Instagram doesn't give you a built-in way to save someone else's Reel — there's no download button anywhere in the app. If you want to keep a copy of a public Reel, you need a separate tool that can pull the video file directly. Here's the fastest way to do it.

Step-by-step: saving a Reel

  1. Open the Reel you want to save and tap the paper-plane "Share" icon below it.
  2. Tap "Copy Link" — this puts the Reel's direct URL on your clipboard.
  3. Paste the link into the Instagram Reels Downloader.
  4. Tap Download. The tool resolves the original MP4 file, sound included, at the same resolution it was uploaded in.
  5. Save it to your device — no login, no watermark, no app to install.

Why not just screen record?

Screen recording works, but it re-encodes the video through your device's screen and microphone pipeline, so you lose quality and can pick up UI elements or notification pop-ups in the frame. Pulling the original file instead keeps the source resolution and audio track intact.

Does this work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — since it runs entirely in the browser, the same steps work on iPhone and Android, as well as desktop. There's nothing to install either way.
