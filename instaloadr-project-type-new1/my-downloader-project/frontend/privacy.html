When you paste a link into InstaLoadr, we send it to our backend to resolve the media it points to. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your Instagram username or password.

Cookies & analytics

InstaLoadr does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies, and does not run any third-party tracking scripts. If that changes, this section will be updated to name the specific tool and link to its own privacy policy — and the "Last updated" date above will move.

Third-party content

Media fetched through InstaLoadr comes directly from Instagram's own content delivery network. We do not store copies of downloaded media on our servers.

Your choices
