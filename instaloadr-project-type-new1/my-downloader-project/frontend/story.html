Instagram Story Downloader

Why Stories need a different approach

Unlike a regular post, a Story link stops resolving the moment it expires — there's no way around that, and there shouldn't be. Save it while it's live, or not at all. If you're after a permanent post or a Reel instead, try the Photo downloader or the Reels downloader.

Features of the Story downloader

Paste a link to a live Story and InstaLoadr resolves whichever media is behind it — a photo, a video, or a multi-segment Story — and lists each item with its own Download button. There's no need to time a screen recording to the Story's autoplay; the tool grabs the file directly, in whatever the few seconds you have before it expires.

Benefits over screen-recording a Story

Screen-recording a Story on a phone means capturing your whole screen, including the progress bar at the top and the reply box at the bottom, then trimming that afterward. Resolving the source file skips all of it — no UI in frame, no re-encoding, and no need to catch the exact right moment before the Story advances to the next segment or closes.

HD download quality

Story photos and videos save at the resolution Instagram stored them in, not a screen-captured, re-compressed copy. That matters more for Stories than regular posts, since a lot of Story content is shared once and never reposted anywhere else — a clean, full-quality copy is often the only one that will exist.

Supported formats

Story photos save as JPG and Story videos save as MP4 with audio, matching whatever format the original upload used. Multi-segment Stories are split so each segment downloads as its own file rather than one merged clip.

If a Story won't load

The two most common reasons are timing and access: the Story already expired before the link was pasted, or the account posting it is private. Unlike a regular post, there's no way to re-fetch an expired Story — if the 24-hour window has closed, the link simply won't resolve anymore, regardless of how it's pasted.

Saving a Story with multiple segments

When someone posts several Story segments back to back, InstaLoadr lists each one it can find as its own item with its own Download button, rather than merging them into a single file. That keeps each photo or clip at its own original length and quality instead of stitching them together.

Stories with stickers, polls, or music

Interactive elements like polls, question boxes, or a music sticker are part of how a Story displays inside the app, but they don't change the underlying photo or video file — that's what gets saved. If a Story used a music sticker, the audio is generally part of the video itself once it's exported, so it saves along with the rest of the clip rather than as a separate track.

A closer look at the 24-hour window

How to Download Instagram Stories

Stories disappear after 24 hours, so save a copy before it expires by following these steps:

  1. Open the Story while it's still live. Find it on the account's profile or from your Stories bar.
  2. Tap the Share icon. It's the paper-plane icon in the bottom-right of the screen.
  3. Tap "Copy Link." This copies the Story's direct URL to your clipboard.
  4. Paste the link above. Come back to this page and paste it into the box at the top before the 24-hour window closes.
  5. Tap Download. InstaLoadr checks the Story is still public and active, then resolves the file.
  6. Tap Save. The photo or video downloads to your device at full quality.

The same steps work on iPhone, Android, and desktop browsers — device-specific tips are below if you need them.

Download the Story on any device

  1. Open the post, tap the ⋯ or Share icon, then Copy Link.

  3. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download.
  4. Tap Save, then use the share sheet to save it to your device.

On Android

  1. Open the post and tap Copy Link from the share menu.
  2. Switch to Chrome (or your usual browser) and open this page.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download.
  4. Tap Save — the file lands in your Downloads folder or Gallery.
